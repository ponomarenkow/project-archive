# Shape Recognition with Deep Neural Network

It was my first attempt at using torch and DNN, made at the end of 3rd grade. A model was trained to perform a very simple shape recognition task: whether something is a circle, a triangle or a heart. Training and testing was done on a dataset I draw by hand in Paint. I managed to reach 80-90% accuracy, but neither changing parameters nor the amount of training data significantly affected its performance after that.
